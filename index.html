About · Hello There!

I'm Lara Ortiz-Martin

I am a PhD graduate in Computer Science specializing in system security. And a Front-end Lead Architect with 14+ years of experience building and maintaining complex web apps in the learning and insurance industry.

My research background was focus on the analysis and exploitation of biosignals, particularly electrocardiogram (ECG), and explored the potential of using this kind of signals as a source of randomness for security applications.

As a Frontend Architect I was responsible for designing a high-level architecture, establishing technical guidelines, and ensuring that the system is scalable, accessible, maintainable, and secure.

At the same time I was working in the industry, I have continued my research on topics such as security in browser extensions and the application of large language models in research.
